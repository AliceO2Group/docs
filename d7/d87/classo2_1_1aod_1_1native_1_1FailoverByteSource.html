Detailed Description

The replicas of one file, read as one ByteSource. A read which fails with an IoError is retried on the same replica, reopened, Options::readRetries times with a growing delay, and then on the next replica of the same size. Opening goes through the replicas in order too. Reads already in flight on a replica that is being replaced finish there, or fail and are retried.
