Detailed Description

What the two VecGeom navigators share: the correspondence between a VecGeom navigation state and the Geant4 navigation history, which is the touchable the scoring code reads, and the unit conversion. Geant4 works in millimetres, the VecGeom geometry is converted from TGeo in centimetres; every point crossing this boundary is scaled, directions are not.
