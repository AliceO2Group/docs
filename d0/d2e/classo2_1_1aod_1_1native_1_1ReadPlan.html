Detailed Description

The baskets of the trees a DF is read from, fetched in as few reads as possible: ranges are registered first, and the first lookup of one which is not in memory yet fetches every pending range, merging those closer than Options::coalesceGap. The fetched bytes are held until the plan goes away.
