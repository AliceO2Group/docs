Detailed Description

Primary ionisation in the TRD gas from tabulated collision spectra, independent of the transport engine's energy-loss model. The tables hold the number of collisions per cm with an energy transfer below the delta-ray production cut of the gas (10 keV) and their energy distribution, as functions of beta*gamma, taken from Geant4's photo-absorption ionisation (PAI) cross-section for the Xe/CO2 and Ar/CO2 mixtures.
