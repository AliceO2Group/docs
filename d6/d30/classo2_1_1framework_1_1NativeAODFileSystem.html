◆ planFor()

std::shared_ptr< ReadPlan > o2::framework::NativeAODFileSystem::planFor ( std::string const &  path)

The read plan with the tree at path in it; nullptr when the file is mapped and nothing needs reading. A new DF gets new plans, with the trees read in the previous DF already in them: a reader asks for the same tables in every DF, so the first read of a plan fetches all of its trees together. With Options::bufferBudget, the trees of a DF are split into plans of at most that many bytes, in the order they are read, and a plan is freed once the reads of its trees are done; a tree larger than the budget gets a plan of its own. For a remote file, the next DF is prepared in the background meanwhile (keys, TTrees and data of the same trees, up to the budget), so that its round trips overlap with the decoding of this one.
