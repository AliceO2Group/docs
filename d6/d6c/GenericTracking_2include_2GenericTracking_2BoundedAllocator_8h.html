Detailed Description

Shared budgeted host allocation and bounded-vector utilities.

Reconciles GTRK's overflow-safe allocator, profiling counters and vector rebuilds with the owned-resource, mutable-budget and stage-peak contracts used by the upstream legacy ITS tracker and vertexer.
