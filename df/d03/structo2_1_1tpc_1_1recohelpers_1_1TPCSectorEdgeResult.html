Detailed Description

Result of one TPC row/sector-edge evaluation.

nRows* count accepted row-centre intersections. length*Cm accumulates the corresponding approximate track length as radial row thickness multiplied by ds/dr. The four 152-row masks deliberately retain redundant information so the partition identity can be checked directly.

maxRadiusCm is the radius of the last accepted sampled row; NaN if none. It is not the continuous mathematical turning radius of a helix and is not a propagation-failure sentinel. firstDeadRow and lastDeadRow delimit the first/last crossed rows classified outside the active-pad boundary; they remain -1 when no Dead row is found.
