Detailed Description

Builds an Acts::TrackingGeometry from a TGeo tree.

Implementations are detector specific and live with their detector (see o2::alice3::Gen3BlueprintBuilder). They are injected into TrackingGeometryManager at runtime, so this library never depends on any concrete detector.
