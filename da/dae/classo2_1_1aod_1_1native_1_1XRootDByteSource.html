Detailed Description

A file read with the XRootD client (root://, roots://, xroot://, xroots://). readvInto() sends its ranges as vectored reads for the small ones, within the server's kXR_readv limits, and plain reads of at most 8 MB for the large ones, in rounds of at most Options::remoteInFlightBytes: usually one round trip for a whole read plan, and no request so large that it expires on a slow link. Errors are not retried here: see FailoverByteSource.
